SIGNAL finds where a company's numbers persistently break three expectations — its own history, its peers, and what its management said — and reports a finding only after it survives a pre-registered attempt to disprove it. Find the breach. Trace the evidence. Try to break the hypothesis.

01The problem

A non-obvious finding is, almost by definition, not written down anywhere. Retrieval can only return what someone has already said.

Dashboardsshow headline ratios — not the drivers underneath them.
Summarisersrepeat the narrative; a “critic” prompt argues, it does not test.
SIGNALcomputes where history ≠ peers ≠ narrative, then tries to explain it away.

04Why it is different

Independent evidence

Tested, not argued
